01 · A comprehensive vision

Building
a watch house.

02 · The founding sign

From B
to The arch.

The logo reinterprets the Boanton B in the form of an arch. The repetition of the motif and its symmetrical composition build an architectural emblem.

The drawing refers to the Art Deco fountain decorations of the New York buildings of the 1920s and 1930s: deployment of water jets, repeated curves and geometric rhythm. This reference links the graphic identity with the watchmaking object, in the same combination of rigorous lines and rounded shapes.

03 · A geometric signature

The square
inside the circle.

The square inscribed in the round structure the language of the dials. The straight lines organize the surface while the curves of the cushion case soften the reading.

On Emblem, the nested squares make up an architecture around the center of the needles. The lacquered surface reveals the pattern at the rhythm of the reflections. The ivory, deep blue and emerald green each give a different reading of this same geometry.

On Ellipse, the guilloché decor areas bring relief and accompany the central engraved signature. The work of the surfaces and curves extends the brand's Art Deco references.

Ellipse

Curves & reliefs

An interpretation of the Art Deco combining polished steel cushion case, fluid curves and guilloché decorations. The signature engraved in the center of the dial and the double dome sapphire glass contribute to the work of depth and light. The automatic calibre La Joux-Perret G100 animates the collection.

The collection on Maison Boanton

Emblème

Geometry & lacquering

A dial structured by nested squares, inscribed in a 40 mm cushion case. The ivory lacquer, deep blue or emerald green makes vary the presence of lines and reflections. The reinterpreted leaf needles and the fluted bezel prolong the dialogue between geometric rigor and curves.

The collection on Maison Boanton

Héritage

From car to watchmaking

A translation of the automotive codes in the watchmaker's drawing. The metallic blue of the dial evokes the berlinette, the hands repeat the drawing of a steering wheel and the luminescent arches refer to a luminous signature. The blue alcantara bracelet extends this universe. A limited series of 252 copies.

The collection on Maison Boanton

Pink Edition Emblem

An edition for October Rose

A declination ofEmbleme around a powdery pink lacquer dial, keeping the geometric construction and curves of the collection. Created on the occasion of Rose October, this series of 24 numbered copies supports the association IMAGYN, to which part of the profits is donated.

The edition on Maison Boanton

05 · From sketch to object

Designing
every detail.

The research focuses on proportions, housing profile, horn integration, crown and assembly of components.

The sketches relate the external design and the construction of the watch. They show the research on the curved glass, the bezel, the fixing systems and the possibilities of interchangeability of the bracelets.
