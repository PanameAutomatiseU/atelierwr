Lemarys Paris

From brand mark to object

I developed a leather goods collection around structured silhouettes and sculptural clasps. Leather, gold leaf and stone veneer bring the contrast between refined geometry and raw material into each piece.

The art direction process

I began with references from architecture, fashion, jewellery and materials. The moodboards gave me a direction: the balance between raw and refined. I explored the brand mark alongside the shapes of the bags, then used clasp studies and material trials to connect the visual identity to the collection.

Mulberry

Equestrian elegance

I drew on Mulberry’s British heritage to design four bags for everyday use. Saddle lines, bridles, braided straps and bit-inspired hardware informed the silhouettes and details.

Visual research and moodboards

I explored “Made in England” through equestrian dress, uniforms, Victorian ornament, dandyism and punk. Bringing these references together helped me choose colours, materials and details. Tartan, quilting, braid and cord then found their way into sketches and finishes.
