Brand strategy · Visual identity · Artistic direction

Logo & Identity

& Vision Method

Build a distinctive sign and the universe that makes it live.

A successful identity is not limited to a beautiful logo. It accurately translates the positioning, values and personality of a brand into an immediately recognizable language.

Each creation seeks the balance between singularity, simplicity and ability to deploy. The sign must remain legible at all scales, cross the supports and maintain its consistency over time.

Creative process

Branded DNA
the visual system.

  1. 01
    Understand

    Analyze activity, public, values, uses and competitive environment.

  2. 02
    Explore

    Search for concepts, symbols, typographies and compositions that can incarnate the brand.

  3. 03
    Building

    Specify the sign, its proportions, colors and rules to form a coherent identity.

  4. 04
    Deployment

    Adapt the system to print, digital, signal and product media, and then accompany its use.
