Maison Boanton

From the brand identity to the design of individual watch components, Art Deco references connect the mark, the cushion case and the geometric dials. The collection includes Ellipse, Emblème, Héritage and the Pink Edition.

Cartier

I joined Cartier International as a watch designer in 2009 and led its Paris watch design studio from 2013 to 2018, working with a team of four across watch design, complications, decorative crafts and high jewellery.

Professional archive. This work was created during my employment at Cartier International and predates Atelier WR. Atelier WR is independent of Cartier. Model names, marks and images belong to their respective rights holders.
