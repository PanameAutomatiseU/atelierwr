Heritage luminaire inspired by Art Deco

Govee · Collection 05 · Table lamp

HERITAGE.
Memory
Light.

Heritage transposes the nobility of the great chandeliers of the 1920s and 1930s into a table lamp designed for contemporary interiors.

Its superimposed crowns evoke the cascades of Art Deco crystal and the ceremonial light of theatres and palaces. The project retains this architectural power while bringing it back to an intimate, personal and connected scale.

02 · Research & construction

Crowns
balance.

The research adapts the architecture of the historic chandeliers to a compact silhouette. The successive crowns organize the proportions, while the fluted diffusers recall the transparency and refraction of the crystal. The brushed aluminum rings and rod structure the whole.

03 · Evolution in streetlights

Heritage
High Light.

Heritage changes its scale to become a sculptural floor lamp, designed for the most demanding interiors. The floor-dwelling lamp is worked out of faceted glass crystals, set in a brass structure, in order to multiply the reflections and diffuse a warm, precious and enveloping light.
