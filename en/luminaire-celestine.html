Celestine, lamp inspired by Haute Couture

01 · Creative territory · Haute Couture

Celestine.
Light
dressed.

Inspired by the delicate folds of haute couture and the timeless elegance of precious objects, Celestine transforms the lamp into an interior accessory.

The pleated foot diffuses a living RGBIC light like a veil of colours. The air-shaft brings a warm white light, conducive to relaxation, reading and contemplation. The technology effaces behind the beauty of the gesture and the magic of light.

02 · Research & Mechanism

A silhouette
which adapts.

The shade adjusts in height thanks to a telescopic central rod and a metal cone designed for an intuitive grip. The silhouette evolves from a compact presence to a more slender piece, while the canned foot retains a homogeneous RGBIC diffusion.

03 · Product presentation

A Jewel
in space.

The warm white light envelops the space while the colors circulate in the folds of the foot. Celestine naturally passes from a discreet presence to a centerpiece, combining RGBIC ambiance, decorative refinement and visual comfort.

04 · Option Jewellery pads

Crystal
on the move.

Inspired by the crystal shades of large hotels and Art Deco interiors, this version replaces the luminous disc with a fringe of synthetic crystal pads. Bead chains, supported by two metal rings, retain the telescopic mechanism and capture each ray.

05 · Presentation of the variant

A shrapnel
precious.

In the daytime, the pampilles reveal the finesse of their drawing. At night, they multiply the reflections and create a glamorous, warm and refined atmosphere. Celestine becomes a true luminous jewel where each radiance adds an emotion.
