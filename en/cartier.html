These achievements were developed prior to the creation of Atelier WR, as part of William Rigoulet's salaried duties at Cartier's creation studio in Paris.

William Rigoulet joined Cartier International as a watchmaker in 2009, then became head of the watchmaker's design studio in Paris from 2013 to 2018. He accompanied a team of four people and worked on iconic watches, complications, haute horlogerie, craft and high jewellery.

Development of British Tank & Tank MC

Two complementary developments extend the historical architecture of the Tank. The English Tank works to integrate the bracelet and crown into a fluid silhouette; the Tank MC asserts a more masculine expression around an expanded case and the 1904 MC manufacture movement.

Continuity of the case and bracelet

English Tank

The development of the English Tank is based on a fluid reading of the historical stretchers. The crown and its cabochon are integrated into the side of the case, while the metal bracelet extends directly the rectangular geometry. The dial retains the Roman numerals, the blue sword hands, the railway timer and the date window. The proportions search aim at a present watch, comfortable and immediately identifiable.

Men's architecture and manufacturing movement

Tank MC

The Tank MC expands and extends the proportions of the Tank to build a more masculine presence. Polished stretchers frame a dial structured by Roman numerals, a small second at six o'clock and a date window. Declined in steel or gold, on d-alligator bracelet, it is designed around the automatic calibre manufacture 1904 MC and combines geometric rigor, power and elegance.
