The route

  • 2018 · today

    Atelier WR · Marseille

    Founder and artistic director. Regular customers: Almé Paris, L'Eau à la Bouche, Bonjour Brand.

  • 2022 · today

    Maison Boanton · Paris

    Co-founder of the watch brand. I draw the watches and I hold the identity of the brand.

  • 2024 · 2025

    Almé Paris · Avignon

    Designer accessory collections: leather goods, shoes, belts. New logo and identity redesign.

  • 2009 · 2018

    Cartier International · Paris

    Designer watchmaking, then head of the watchmaking studio (4 people) from 2013 to 2018. From the pillar watches to the high watchmaking.

  • 2008 · 2009

    Calvin Klein · Florence

    Designer leather goods, responsible for the leather collection.

  • 2006 · 2008

    Kipling · Belgium & Bally · Milan

    Designer leather goods and accessories in freelance: women's collections, bags, sneakers, metal elements.

  • 2003 · 2005

    One hundred degrees · Paris & Volkswagen Design Center · Wolfsburg

    Product design, packaging and brand identity, then automotive exterior design.

Training
