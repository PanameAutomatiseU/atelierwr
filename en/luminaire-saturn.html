Saturn, lamp design and luminaires for Govee

01 · Creative territory

A light
which becomes presence.

Saturn transforms ambient light into a sculptural presence. Inspired by the rings of the planet and the imagination of flying objects, the collection combines an opalin dome with an animated RGBIC ring.

The white light brings comfort, while the color draws a mobile and immersive aura around the object. Declined as a table lamp and then as a floor lamp, the same circular signature connects technology, cosmic poetry and daily use.

02 · Table lamp

Saturn
Lamp table.

A stable mineral base carries a luminous disc that seems to float around the central dome. The research explores the proportions, materiality of the foot and the balance between functional light and coloured halo.

03 · Floor lamp

Saturn
Floor Lamp.

The orbital signature changes scale to structure the space. The floor lamp combines indirect ambiance lighting with an orientable reading source, in a vertical, sober and architectural silhouette.
