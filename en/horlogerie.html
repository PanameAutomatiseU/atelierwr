Creative direction · Management · Watchmaking development

An integrated approach to watchmaking.

Former manager of the Cartier design studio in Paris, I have acquired a global vision of the profession and the ability to lead a project from A to Z: from the first sketch to the marketing of the watch. This experience combines creative direction, team management, collection design and coordination of the various partners involved in the development.

This mastery is used to preserve the strength of the concept throughout its transformation into a product. It includes analysis of brand DNA and specifications, creative research, case design, dials, bracelets and details, volume development, exchanges with design offices and manufacturers, prototype monitoring, technical adjustments, final validation and launch preparation.

My knowledge of jewellery and art crafts enriches this approach with an understanding of crimping, stones, engraving, enamelling, lacquering and precious finishes. Used to complex projects, I know how to adapt creation to technical, industrial, economic, calendar and commercial constraints, while maintaining the aesthetic consistency and values of the brand.
