01

A comprehensive approach to creation

My approach links artistic direction, product design, visual identity, graphic design and brand narrative. Each collaboration begins with a meeting, attentive listening and the desire to understand what makes a project truly unique.

From initial intent to realization: positioning, research, creation, development and dialogue with the workshops. This cross-sectional vision creates continuity between the background, form, objects, images and how a brand tells its story.

03

Create in Marseille and the PACA region

Marseille is a living, contrasting and deeply inspiring territory. The city brings together artisans, designers, architects, restaurateurs, hoteliers, artists and entrepreneurs with very different paths.

Working locally gives priority to direct exchanges, visits to workshops, understanding of subjects and a closer relationship with those who manufacture.

I wish to contribute to the valorisation of this know-how and to support projects rooted in their territory, while giving them a strong identity and a wider ambition.

04

Human collaborations

"I'm looking for both human and professional collaborations."

Sincerity, authenticity, respect, curiosity and sharing are at the heart of my way of working. I appreciate the projects carried by committed people, who know the value of time, gesture and work well done.

I am not trying to impose a style. My role is to understand a story, to reveal what makes it special and to give it a fair form. The best collaborations arise from a simple dialogue, a shared trust and a common desire to create something that has meaning.
