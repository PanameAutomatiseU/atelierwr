I’m William Rigoulet, a designer and art director based in Marseille. I work with brands to connect their identity with the objects they make, from the first visual research to the details of the finished design.

My practice spans leather goods, watchmaking, jewellery, furniture, packaging and visual identity. Nine years in Cartier’s watch design studio, five of them leading it, shaped my approach to precision, materials and detail.
