Atelier WR is the Marseille studio of William Rigoulet, designer and art director. I help brands carry a coherent idea from their visual identity through to their products, bringing together art direction, design, storytelling and development.

Every project begins with a story worth telling. I look at its context, heritage, uses, craft and the brand’s ambitions. That story guides decisions about form, materials, detail, packaging, imagery and how the product is presented.

I work across watchmaking, jewellery, leather goods, accessories, furniture, packaging and visual identity. My role spans positioning and early research, design refinement and renderings, and working with workshops through development.

Nine years in Cartier’s watch design studio, five of them leading it, shaped my approach to precision, materials and detail. Drawing, painting and photography continue to inform how I see colour, light and composition.
