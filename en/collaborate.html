01

An integrated approach to design

My work brings together art direction, product design, visual identity, graphic design and brand storytelling. I start each collaboration by listening and getting to know what makes the project distinctive.

I work from the initial idea through to production, covering positioning, research, design and development in dialogue with workshops. This allows the idea to carry through to the objects, images and the way the brand tells its story.

03

Designing in Marseille and the surrounding region

Marseille is a lively city full of contrasts. It brings together makers, designers, architects, restaurateurs, hoteliers, artists and entrepreneurs from very different backgrounds.

Working locally means meeting in person, visiting workshops, getting to know the materials and working closely with the people who make things.

I want to help bring attention to these skills and give locally rooted projects a strong identity and the scope to reach a wider audience.

04

Working relationships

“The people I meet matter to me as much as the work we do together.”

Honesty, respect, curiosity and a willingness to share are central to how I work. I enjoy working with people who understand the time, care and skill that go into making something well.

My role is to understand the story of a project, find what makes it distinctive and give it a form that feels right. The best collaborations grow out of an open conversation, mutual trust and a shared reason for making something.

06

Who I work with

I work with local makers and workshops, design houses, architects, interior designers, hotels, restaurants, cultural venues, emerging brands, entrepreneurs and local institutions.

Each collaboration can take a different form, from a one-off commission or a full design brief to art direction or ongoing conversations about the development of a brand.
