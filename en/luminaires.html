Luminaires

Light design · Coloured atmosphere · Technology

Luminaires & bright experiences

For Govee, a brand specializing in ambient light and colour, I develop several artistic orientations capable of transforming light technology into identifiable objects, desirable and suitable for domestic use.

Each collection starts from a clean visual narrative, then declines this language into silhouettes, effects of light, materials and complementary typologies. Saturn explores the orbital imaginary. Aura reinterprets the Space Age. Canopée translates the protective forms of living into an organic light presence. LUMA revisits the traditional lantern through the smoked glass and an immersive light. Heritage transposes the monumental elegance of Art Deco chandeliers into a connected table lamp. Celestine dresses the light of the pleats of haute couture and an option of jewellery pads.
