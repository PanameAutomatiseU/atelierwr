LUMA, contemporary lantern design

Govee · Collection 04 · Lantern lamp

LUMA.
Reinvent
The lantern.

LUMA reinterprets the reassuring warmth of the traditional lantern in a contemporary object where light becomes an immersive presence.

The project retains the primary function of the lantern and the shade: to protect, diffuse and magnify the light. Its language combines a protective dome, fluid lines and RGBIC technology that transforms the atmosphere without taking the step on the object.

02 · Research & development

Protect,
Broadcast, magnify.

The large smoked glass dome forms a protective envelope and lets the light float inside. Research refines the proportions of the dome, the organic curve of the base and the fine veins inspired by the Space Age design and neo-futuristic architecture.

03 · Product presentation

LUMA
in situation.

Turns off, LUMA looks like a minimalist sculpture. The colorful light diffuses behind the smoky glass and sets up a soft, enveloping and contemplative atmosphere in contemporary habitat.

04 · Materials & finishes

A variant
more graphic.

The black variant reinforces the dialogue between matter and light. The transparent polycarbonate treated with anti-reflectiveness, the dark fluted structure and the opalin diffuser give more depth to the RGBIC gradient, while the light base highlights the silhouette.
