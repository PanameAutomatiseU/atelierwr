Furniture & Objects

Creative direction · Product design · Development

Furniture & Objects

I design objects and collections of furniture from their context, their uses and the identity of the place or brand. The process combines analysis, search for forms and volumes, sketches, functional development, choice of materials, rendering and preparation of the elements necessary for development.

Each project seeks a balance between aesthetic presence, comfort, feasibility and experience of use, from the initial concept to the definition of a coherent and identifiable proposal.

Project 01 · Artistic direction · Design furniture

Grand Palace.
Furniture & Seats.

This research brings together a family of furniture designed to accompany the uses of a monumental place. Sittings, sofas and outdoor furniture interact with the architecture of the Grand Palais with sober lines, generous volumes and a mastered graphic presence. From the collection drawing to the situations, the project builds a coherent, comfortable and adaptable ensemble to the various spaces and events.

Project 02 · Design of object · Connected piano

Piano Carod.
The sound's getting better.

Carod is a connected piano from a Chinese manufacture inspired by Italian Gothic architecture and its aesthetic codes. Its vertical lines, rhythms and details evoke the structure of a nave, while the digital integration inscribed the instrument in a contemporary use. When the sound takes shape and the shape evokes the harmony of sound, the object finds its balance between heritage and technology.

Project 03 · Modular furniture · Compact housing

Ere of me.
Playground.

Thought for the reduced space of a student studio, Era of I transforms a compact volume into a real home play area. Only one set is deployed in sofa, reception space, group sitting or two places. The project favours modularity, simplicity of use, user friendliness and controlled cost in order to offer flexible furniture that adapts to the moments of the day as well as to the dimensions of the place.
