Canopy, lamp inspired by vegetable forms

Govee · Collection 03 · Office lamp

Canopy.
A light
protective.

Canopey draws on the soothing forms of living to create a warm halo at 360°, soft as a light filtered by foliage.

Its silhouette combines the fungus, tree and natural textures of stems, shells and petals. The round and generous volumes of design of the 1960s and 1970s are reinterpreted in a contemporary, organic and sculptural writing.

02 · Research & development

Live
in shape.

The research balances a broad and welcoming shade with a fluted base inspired by stems and vegetal veins. The diffuser ensures 360° ambient light, complemented by soft RGB lighting, a fine edge and an integrated button.

03 · Product presentation

Canopy
in situation.

Designed for living and working spaces, Canopée diffuses a soft, enveloping light that is conducive to concentration and calmness. Its shade seems to float above the base and installs a familiar, almost natural presence in space.
