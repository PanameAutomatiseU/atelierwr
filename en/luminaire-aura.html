Aura, concept of table lamp for Govee

Govee · Collection 02 · Table lamp

Aura.
Light, shaped
by emotion.

Aura does not just light up: it transforms light into presence and defines the atmosphere of a space.

Inspired by the optimism of Space Age design of the 1960s and the sculptural forms of Italian radical design, Aura reinterprets the familiar silhouette of the mushroom lamp. Its Sienna Earth colour, its white diffuser and its RGBIC light lines make up an object that is warm, technological and timeless. Turns off, Aura is a sculpture. Turns on, it becomes a luminous material.

02 · Research & development

Influence
the concept.

The search translates the curves of the Space Age furniture into a compact and immediately identifiable silhouette. The proportions of the dome and the organic base are refined with the opalin diffuser, the RGBIC light under the shade and the luminous edge that highlights the base.

03 · Product presentation

Aura
in situation.

The staging presents Aura in a contemporary interior at dawn. The soft light structure the volume of the lamp while the color RGBIC accompanies the atmosphere and the different uses of space.
