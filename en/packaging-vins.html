Artistic Direction · Boxes · Labels · Range Identities

Packaging & wines

& Vision Method

Design a box that gives value to the product.

Packaging links the brand, product and discovery gesture. Its structure, graphics and materials must protect, inform and create an immediately identifiable experience.

The initial positioning to be developed with the manufacturers: range architecture, volume searches, labels, illustrations, choice of finishes, prototypes and preparation of production elements.

Key competencies
  1. 01Brand analysis & specifications
  2. 02Range architecture
  3. 03Volume, usage & structure
  4. 04Graphic Design, Illustration & Label
  5. 05Materials, finishes & prototypes
  6. 06Manufacturer Development & Follow-up
Design process

Of intent
production.

  1. 01
    Position

    Clarify the product, market, uses, range and expected experience.

  2. 02
    Designing

    Explore structures, volumes, opening gestures, graphic compositions and brand signatures.

  3. 03
    Prototype

    Test the proportions, materials, assemblies, finishes and perception of the product.

  4. 04
    Manufacturing

    Prepare plans and files, dialogue with manufacturers and track adjustments until validation.
