Interstice Collection · Jewellery

Geometry
on the move.

The Interstice collection explores a geometrical vocabulary built around the cut-paned square and the space between the lines. The same module is transformed into a link, frame, crimping or openwork structure. Declined into bracelets, necklaces, rings and earrings, the collection seeks a balance between architectural rigour, visual lightness and wear comfort.

  1. 01
    Search & drawing

    The first sketches set out the intention, proportions and manner in which the jewel accompanies the body. The repetition of the octagonal module, its rhythm and its joints build a coherent family of pieces.

  2. 02
    Mockups & Decline

    The drawings are developed in detailed mockups to test volumes, openwork, metal colours and stone place. These views allow comparison of compositions before starting the development.

  3. 03
    Technical development

    The model chosen is specified with the manufacturer and the craftsmen: construction, joints, fastening systems, thicknesses, crimping and finishing.

  4. 04
    Prototype & Creation

    The prototypes allow to adjust the range, balance and perceived quality. The final part retains the strength of the original design while meeting the requirements of manufacture and use.

02 · Mockups from the collection

Module
To the jewel.

Geometric collection

An identity built by the module.

The mockups translate the octagonal frame into rings, earrings, bracelets and pendants. Each proposal examines the rhythm of fullness and voids, the scale ratios and the continuity of the pattern around the body.

These situations allow you to compare the finishes, the metal colours and the place of the stones before the technical development. The transition from the drawing to the mockup also checks the wear, the visual balance and the consistency of the entire collection.

03 · Jewellery application

Card holders
secure.

A search for precious decorations applied to a secure card holder. The compositions combine coloured lacquer, polished metal and diamond punctuations to transform a technological object into a luxury accessory. Two variations explore a deep green palette, red and black, then a black and white contrast, while respecting the constraints of opening, thickness and protection of the cards.
